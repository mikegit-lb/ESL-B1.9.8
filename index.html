S4 — THE BRIDGE TASK: "The Bad Day" (The Lead-in to Past Perfect)

⚠️ Mystery Problem Task 👥 1:1 Interaction ⏱️ 5 Minutes

Tell the story of Aylin at Kadıköy ferry station using Past Simple only:

Card 1: Aylin woke up late and ran.
Card 2: The ferry departed (left).
Card 3: Aylin arrived at gate at 8:15.
Card 4: Aylin lost her travel card.

Try telling it: "Aylin arrived at the station and the ferry left. She lost her card..."

❓ The Problem: Did Aylin catch the ferry? Or was it already gone when she arrived? If you only use Past Simple, nobody knows what happened first!
B1. The Hook: Solving Aylin's Bad Day The Solution

In S4, our sentence was ambiguous:

❌ "When Aylin arrived at the station, the ferry left."
(Ambiguous: Did she see it? Did she catch it? We don't know!)
✅ "When Aylin arrived at the station, the ferry had left."
(Crystal clear: The ferry left at 8:10. Aylin arrived at 8:15. The departure happened FIRST.)

B5. Minimal Pairs: How Grammar Changes the Story Meaning Contrast

Pair 1: The Coffee

• When I arrived, Deniz made coffee. ➔ I arrived first. Then Deniz made coffee.

• When I arrived, Deniz had made coffee. ➔ Deniz made coffee first. A warm cup was ready for me!

Pair 2: The Classroom

• When the class finished, Murat left. ➔ The bell rang, and Murat walked out.

• When the class finished, Murat had left. ➔ Murat walked out earlier before the bell rang.

Pair 3: The Shopping

• I bought the coat when I saw the price. ➔ I saw the price tag, liked it, and bought it.

• I had bought the coat when I saw the sale flyer. ➔ I bought it earlier at full price; later saw the sale.

B6. Common Mistakes & Turkish L1 Interference Guide Alerts

1. ❌ Dropping 'had': "When I got home, my brother went to bed."

✅ "When I got home, my brother had gone to bed."

🇹🇷 Turkish Interference Note: Turkish marks past time with -di for both actions (e.g. "Eve vardığımda kardeşim uyumuştu / uyudu"). Turkish learners frequently forget to use had. Error Tag: [F].

2. ❌ Wrong Participle: "Selin was sad because she had lose her ticket."

✅ "Selin was sad because she had lost her ticket."

Rule: You must always use the Past Participle (V3) after had, never the base form. Error Tag: [P].

3. ❌ 'have/has' instead of 'had': "Before the film began, we have bought our food."

✅ "Before the film began, we had bought our food."

Rule: The auxiliary for past perfect is always had, never have/has. Error Tag: [F].

4. ❌ Reversing the Sequence: "After the bus arrived, we had taken our bags."

✅ "After we had taken our bags, the bus arrived."

Rule: The earlier event takes past perfect; the later event takes past simple. Error Tag: [S].
